Chicken Cordon Bleu

Description

Crisp on the outside and tender on the inside, this classic Chicken Cordon Bleu features juicy chicken breasts stuffed with savory ham and melty Swiss cheese, coated in golden breadcrumbs, and baked or pan-fried to perfection.

Finished with a rich, creamy Dijon mustard sauce, it delivers restaurant-quality flavor that's impressive enough for special occasions yet easy enough for a weeknight dinner.

Ingredients

Steps
  • 2 pcs Boneless chicken breast
  • 2 pcs sliced ham
  • 2 pcs sliced cheese
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup flour
  • 1 cup bread crumbs
  • Salt
  • Ground black pepper
  • Cooking oil
  1. Pound chicken breast until ¼ inch in thickness. Rub both sides of chicken with salt.
  2. Top chicken breast with slice ham and cheese then roll the chicken. Tightly wrap chicken in plastic wrap to shape it, twist remaining plastic on the sides and tie it. Chill in the ref for 30 minutes to 1 hour. Or you can use toothpick to fasten the chicken.
  3. In separate serving dish season flour, egg and bread crumbs with salt and pepper. Mix well.
  4. Then dredge rolled chicken in flour, coat in beaten egg and roll in bread crumbs.
  5. Heat enough cooking oil to deep fry in a pan and fry breaded rolled chicken until golden brown. Drain excess oil in paper towel. Sliced chicken and it’s ready to serve!
